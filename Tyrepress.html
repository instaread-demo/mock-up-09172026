Transense signs £700,000 Continental smart tyre tool contract

Date: 15th September 2026 Author: Chris Anthony Comments: 0

Transense Technologies plc’s Translogik division has made a development and supply deal with Continental for a “next-generation digital tyre management tool”.

Under the agreement, Translogik will develop a bespoke version of its handheld TLGX device, incorporating specific enhanced functionality to meet Continental’s operational requirements.

Subject to completion, the six-month funded programme is expected to generate more than £700,000 a year for Transense while strengthening Translogik’s position in digital tyre management, traceability and connected maintenance.

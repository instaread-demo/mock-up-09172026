A brand-new restaurant and lounge is coming to Disney World’s BoardWalk on October 1st! Hurly-Burly offers live entertainment, vintage drinks, and light bites.

We’ve been checking out construction progress on Hurly-Burly, and when we stopped by today, it had a brand-new sign!

We stopped by and had to take pictures of this new sign. These block letters are a vintage red with golden lightbulbs around the edges. This will look so cool lit up at night!

They’ve also installed the red fabric awning covers since the last time we stopped by. The scalloped edges feature golden seashells and dots that are 1920s meets nautical!

Additionally, the bar counter has been installed, but it’s blocked by bushes until Hurly-Burly opens. There’s a new sign that says “Drinks Here!” pointing towards the drink pick-up window.

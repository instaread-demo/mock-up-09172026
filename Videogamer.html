Curse of Resthaven developer Hilltop Studios has revealed the full voice cast for its upcoming gothic narrative roguelite, bringing together actors with credits spanning Star Trek, Assassin’s Creed, Mass Effect, Death Stranding and Cuphead.

The Toronto-based studio has assembled 11 actors who have recorded almost 7,000 lines of dialogue across 25 characters, with four performers also returning from Hilltop’s acclaimed debut game Lil’ Guardsman.

Curse of Resthaven puts players in charge of a struggling island colony as newly appointed governor Ambrose. Governing the place is only part of the problem: Ambrose must also investigate the disappearance of his niece, a mysterious plague and the death of his predecessor, while trapped inside a six-day time loop that inevitably ends in a cosmic catastrophe.

Gameplay combines town management and trading with card-based expeditions and interrogations, meaning its large cast of fully voiced characters should have plenty of opportunities to make an impression. Every resident of Resthaven has their own motivations and secrets, leaving players to work out who — or potentially what — can be trusted.

Leading the cast is Raoul Bhaneja, who plays disgraced war hero General Oliver Bolingbroke. Wade Bogert-O’Brien voices protagonist Governor Ambrose, Tracy Michailidis plays Iryna, Jan Alexandra Smith voices Evelyn Sprigg, and Alana Bridgewater plays Lydia Braithwaite.

Game director Scott Christian said voice acting had been an important part of Lil’ Guardsman and that the team wanted to push things considerably further for its second game.

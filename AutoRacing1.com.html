Formula 2 News: Freddie Slater to race in with Invicta Racing in 2027

Audi Driver Development Programme driver Freddie Slater (pictured) will be making the step to the FIA Formula 2 Championship with Invicta Racing in 2027, as he continues his progression through the single-seater ranks.

The 18-year-old Briton was announced as the first driver signed to the Audi Driver Development Programme ahead of the 2026 season. Entering the FIA Formula 3 Championship with Trident Motorsport, Slater quickly established himself among the frontrunners and remained in the title fight throughout the year, eventually finishing runner-up after a standout rookie season with one feature race victory, three pole positions and eight podium finishes.

The move to Formula 2 with Invicta Racing sees Slater join a team with a strong recent record in the championship, including consecutive Drivers’ and Teams’ titles in 2024 and 2025. He gets his first opportunity to work with the team during the Formula 2 post-season test.

Slater’s rise through the junior categories has been rapid: after moving into car racing in 2022 he became the youngest-ever Ginetta Junior Winter Series Champion, won the Ginetta Junior Championship the following year, claimed both the Italian and UAE Formula 4 titles in 2024, then won the Formula Regional European Championship in 2025.

He will continue to receive support from the Audi Driver Development Programme throughout his 2027 campaign.

Wilkes-Barre, Luzerne Co. — Wilkes-Barre City police are investigating a car vs pedestrian crash that left one woman seriously injured Monday morning.

Police responded to the intersection of N. Wilkes Barre Blvd and Butler Street around 5:30 a.m. for the report of a pedestrian hit by a car.

Upon arrival police located the woman with a compound fracture to her left arm and visible head trauma laying in the east bound lane of Butler Street.

The woman was taken to Geisinger Wyoming Valley where she needed emergency surgery and is in critical condition.

The driver of the car who struck the woman remained on scene.

Police say the driver consented to a preliminary breath test and volunteered to go to the hospital for a blood draw to rule out DUI.

The intersection of the crash was shut down for mapping and reconstruction of the accident.

Police are continuing to investigate.

Yamaha B200A Dolby Atmos Soundbar Explained: Price, Specs

Yamaha announced the B200A today, calling it its most compact Dolby Atmos soundbar to date. At 23.5 inches wide and 2.5 inches high, it’s intended for space-constrained setups where a larger bar may be impractical.

The B200A is scheduled to ship in November 2026 at $249.95, with a built-in subwoofer so buyers can get Dolby Atmos sound without adding a separate subwoofer, Yamaha said.

Compact Atmos bars from Sonos, Sony and Denon have previously sold in the $399 to $499 range, with the Denon Home 550 sometimes reaching $699. That puts the B200A’s launch price below the historical prices cited for those three competing models. Pricing on those bars fluctuates, so it isn’t a clean discount comparison, and no independent reviews were available at the time of the announcement.

Yamaha B200A price and release date

Yamaha’s consumer audio director framed the B200A around a specific kind of buyer, saying it was designed for people who want better sound without giving up valuable space, and that it is meant for a bedroom, apartment, home office or gaming setup.

The announcement emphasizes those environments rather than a larger living-room system, listing them directly among its stated use cases alongside the same $249.95 price and November 2026 ship date.

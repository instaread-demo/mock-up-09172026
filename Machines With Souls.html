Almost every new performance car sold has adjustable drive modes. They usually don’t work all that well — making the car stiffer, harder, rougher, but not really adding any kind of performance or feel. You set them once, and then never touch the button again. It makes me wonder: who doesn’t want all the performance all the time in their performance car?

Don’t you want it like this AP1 Honda S2000?

Get One
  • Screaming eagle engine
  • Wonderful shifter
  • Aged well, hasn’t it?
Don’t Get One
  • Does tend to bite more than the AP2
  • Still cramped
  • You have to enjoy the work to enjoy the car

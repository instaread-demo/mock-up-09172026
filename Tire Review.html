BKT to Showcase VF Agricultural Tires at Canada’s Outdoor Farm Show

The exhibit will feature AGRIMAX tires designed for soil tillage, harvesting and agricultural material handling.

BKT will display its latest Very High Flexion tire solutions Sept. 15-17 at Canada’s Outdoor Farm Show. The BKT VF tires will be on display in area 751 in Woodstock, Ontario. The exhibit will feature AgriMax tires designed for soil tillage, harvesting and agricultural material handling.

BKT VF Tires Cover Tractors and Harvest Equipment

Featured products include AgriMax V-Flecto for high-horsepower tractors. BKT will also display AgriMax ProHarvest for combines and other harvesting machinery. For tractors used in row crops or spraying, the company will show AgriMax ProCrop.

The VF tires use a large contact patch to manage loads and pressures. The design is intended to help reduce soil compaction while supporting field efficiency and productivity.

VF Technology Targets Load Capacity and Inflation Pressure

According to BKT, its VF tires can provide up to 40% more load capacity at the same inflation pressure. They can also operate at up to 40% lower inflation pressure while carrying the same load.

These features are also available on AgriMax Spargo and V-Flexa. Both models will be displayed at the show. BKT will also feature RideMax FL 693 M for users focused on on-road transport applications.

BKT to Display ATV and Turf Tires

BKT will also showcase tires for all-terrain sports and golf applications. Models on display will include the W 207, AT 108 and Sierra Max.

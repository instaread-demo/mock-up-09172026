PDRA World Finals crowns champions at VMP this Weekend

🕑 September 15, 2026 👤 Anne Proffit 📁 Drag Race 101 - How-Tos, Installs, Tech Tips, News, Racing 💬 0
PDRA’s World Finals take place this week at Virginia Motorsports Park

The 2026 Red Line Oil PDRA Drag Racing Series titles will be decided this coming weekend, when the seventh — and final — race of the season takes place at Virginia Motorsports Park in Dinwiddie, Virginia. The Pro Line Racing Brian Olson Memorial World Finals presented by ProCharger is set for September 17-20 and includes outlying activities that salute the history of the sport, in addition to crowning series champions on the dragstrip at VMP.

Series director Tyler Crossnoe said the season closes out in a big way, pointing to the bonuses on offer in Thursday’s all-star race and the championship battles expected to come down to the wire late on Sunday. He also called it one of the most fan-focused events on the schedule, citing free Thursday admission, a $30 pass covering the rest of the weekend, and a Night of Fire featuring nostalgia Funny Cars, a wheelstander and jet cars.

The weekend’s activities include Thursday’s sixth annual Summit Racing Equipment PDRA ProStars, an all-star specialty event that offers free entry fees to competitors, big prize money and bragging rights for the driver who races their way into the Quick 8-style shootouts.

Once Sunday rolls around, competitors across PDRA’s professional, sportsman and Jr. Dragster classes will determine the 2026 world champions in each of their categories — and with this condensed season, the championship battles are among the closest in series history.

Once Friday qualifying wraps up, a local band performs live at the Racer Party as a season finale celebration. Saturday night is VMP’s Night of Fire, a highlight of these four days of action in the Virginia countryside.

Pro 632 points leader JC Beattie Jr. – Tara Bowker PDRA photo

Two drivers have dominated Pro 632 this season. One won the first two races of the year, adding a third win at the sixth race on tour to build a strong points lead; the other has a first career win and a single final-round finish, and needs to out-qualify the leader by two or more spots to keep the hunt alive.

In Super Street, the driver who finished second in the 2025 championship chase has won the two most recent contests to give himself three wins in four straight final rounds. The defending class champ needs him to fall early to keep his own hopes of a second straight title alive.

Super Street points leader Austin Vincent in his Mustang – Tara Bowker PDRA photo

In Pro Boost, Johnny Camp leads the points and is looking for his first world championship, having set a class E.T. record in the season opener and won the most recent race to build a 10-point cushion. His closest challenger is aiming to become the first driver in series history to win five world championships.

There will be a first-time champion in the Pro Street class, with six drivers still in the running and just one round separating the top two. Two drivers have dominated Pro 632 this season, while in Super Street the points leader has won the two most recent contests to take three wins in four straight final rounds.

Pre-race testing starts at 9AM Thursday, with ProStars driver intros at 4:30 and eliminations beginning at 6PM. Friday qualifying kicks off with Jr. Dragsters at 9AM and includes two pro qualifying sessions. Saturday features the Night of Fire plus a qualifying session and the first round of eliminations. Eliminations conclude on Sunday, with winners crowned in the FuelTech Winners Circle.

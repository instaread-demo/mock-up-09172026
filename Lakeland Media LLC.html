(Litchfield MN-) A McLeod County Deputy will be in court Tuesday on two felony counts of 2nd Degree Assault and Threats for allegedly holding a knife to the throat of a man at a bar in Grove City. The criminal complaint says the 46-year-old Grove City man got into a fight with another man at the bar, then took out his pocketknife, held it against the man’s throat and threatened everyone in the bar.

The alleged incident occurred April 4th after the bar owner told him to leave because he was acting aggressively. She said she knew he was in law enforcement and hesitated to report it, but finally contacted Meeker County authorities about a month later. The incident was captured on video. He was summoned to appear in court rather than arrested, and is not listed as an inmate in the Meeker County Jail. His court appearance is an omnibus hearing to deal with various legal issues connected with the case.
